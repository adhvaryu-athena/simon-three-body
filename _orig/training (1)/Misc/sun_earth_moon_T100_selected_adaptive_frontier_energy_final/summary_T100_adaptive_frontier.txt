Interpretation guide:
- RMS0T is the paper-consistent time-averaged RMS: sqrt(mean_t delta(t)^2).
- Emax is max_t |(E(t)-E(0))/E(0)| in percent, using the same softened-energy convention as the corrected energy-drift scaling script.
- Speed is ias15 runtime divided by SIMON runtime; speed < 1 means SIMON is slower.
- bounded uses the paper's global criterion: all bodies remain within 10 AU of the COM.
- lunar indicates whether the Earth-Moon distance remains within a broad factor-of-two band around the ias15 Earth-Moon range.
- outcome is the reporting-safe classification: Valid, Bounded/degraded, Lunar orbit lost, or Ejected.
- A rollout can be globally bounded but still lose the lunar bound state; this is why both bounded and lunar are reported.
- This real Sun-Earth-Moon case is a physical-consistency and timestep-frontier test, not a guaranteed speedup case.
- Because the Earth-Moon pair is always below the adaptive threshold, adaptive ON can be slower even when stable.
- Long-horizon failed rollouts are stopped once any body exceeds the 10 AU boundedness threshold; remaining samples are filled with the final state to avoid unwritten zero-sample artifacts.
