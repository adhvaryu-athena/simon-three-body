PAPER PARAGRAPH (use only if agreement and speedup are acceptable):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
To test whether the speed--accuracy behaviour observed on IC1
generalises beyond a single hand-selected case, we constructed a
moderate-scattering stress population of 500 compact three-body
configurations using only pre-integration geometric filters. Masses
were sampled log-uniformly from [0.50--2.0],
[0.005--0.50], and
[0.001--0.10]~M_{\odot}, keeping all bodies
inside the scalar network's training range. The stress filter required
negative total energy, safe initial separations, and an estimated outer
periapsis inside the inner interaction zone, so the sample is designed
to test moderate three-body scattering rather than broad hierarchical
population performance.
At $dt=0.08$~yr, SIMON and \texttt{ias15} agreed on the
bounded/ejected classification for 66.6% of configurations
(\texttt{ias15} ejection rate 28.6%; SIMON ejection
rate 50.4%). For the 219 configurations
bounded in both solvers, the mean divergence-rate proxy was
$\lambda = 0.046 \pm 0.039$~yr$^{-1}$ and the
mean final RMS separation from \texttt{ias15} was
1.478 \pm 1.556~AU. The mean per-IC
speed ratio relative to \texttt{ias15} was 0.31\times
(median 0.28\times; population total 0.31\times).
