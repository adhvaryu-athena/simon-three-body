COMPACT ECCENTRIC TRIPLE NN-VALUE EXPERIMENT
==============================================================================

Purpose:
  Test whether SIMON's NN correction improves trajectory accuracy, not stability, in a three-body rollout where the NN is repeatedly active.

Design:
  ias15 reference vs SIMON with NN vs No-NN baseline.
  Adaptive sub-stepping and analytic force direction remain ON for both SIMON variants.
  The only model difference is c predicted by NN vs c fixed to 1.
