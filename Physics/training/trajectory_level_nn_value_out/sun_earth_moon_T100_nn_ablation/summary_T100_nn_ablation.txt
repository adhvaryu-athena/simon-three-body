Interpretation guide:
- RMS0T is sqrt(mean_t delta(t)^2), where delta(t) is RMS position error vs ias15.
- close is the fraction of pair evaluations with r < 0.15 AU.
- NN is the fraction of pair evaluations where the NN correction was accepted and applied.
- fallback is the fraction of pair evaluations using exact Newtonian fallback inside the close region.
- Both SIMON and No-NN retain adaptive stepping and analytic force direction.
- If SIMON is not better than No-NN here, the real Sun-Earth-Moon system should remain a real-data validation rather than an NN-value claim.
